# Urban Housing Lease (Contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda urbana)

Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written in English under Colombian law (Ley 820 de 2003). Every person, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

- Lease id: co-lease-02
- Jurisdiction: Colombia
- Signed on: 2026-07-15

## Parties

Landlord (arrendador): Inversiones Guayacán Malva S.A.S., which receives notices at Avenida de los Sietecueros No. 21-07, office 1102, Medellín.

Tenant (arrendatario): Samuel Arboleda Kowalczyk, who receives notices at the leased apartment.

## Clause 1. Property

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the apartment at Calle de los Carboneros Azules No. 44-18, apartment 803, Medellín, for use as the Tenant's home.

## Clause 2. Term

The contract lasts twelve months, from 1 August 2026 to 31 July 2027, and is extended for equal periods unless either party gives notice as the law provides.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is COP 2,600,000 a month, paid within the first five days of each month.

## Clause 4. Value of the apartment

For this contract, the parties record that the apartment's commercial value is COP 220,000,000 and that its current cadastral appraisal (avalúo catastral) is COP 95,000,000.

## Clause 5. Rent increases

Every twelve months under the same rent, the Landlord may raise the rent by up to the increase in the consumer price index for the previous calendar year, after notifying the Tenant of the amount and its start date through an authorised postal service.

## Clause 6. Utilities

The Tenant pays the public utilities and the building's common fees.

## Clause 7. Use

The apartment is to be used only as the Tenant's home and may not be sublet without the Landlord's written consent.

## Clause 8. Repairs

The Landlord carries out the repairs needed to keep the apartment fit for use. The Tenant pays for minor repairs due to ordinary use.

## Clause 9. Early termination by the Landlord

During any extension of this contract, the Landlord may end it by giving the Tenant 30 days' written notice, without paying any compensation.

## Clause 10. Early termination by the Tenant

The Tenant may end this contract during its initial term or any extension by giving the Landlord at least three months' written notice through an authorised postal service and paying compensation equal to three months' rent.

## Signatures
