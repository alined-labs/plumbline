# Residential Tenancy Contract

Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written under the law of the Emirate of Dubai (Law No. (26) of 2007, as amended by Law No. (33) of 2008). Every person, company, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

- Lease id: dubai-lease-02
- Jurisdiction: Dubai, United Arab Emirates
- Signed on: 2026-03-01

## Parties

Landlord: Idris Brightwater-Nasr (an individual owner).

Tenant: Kwabena Lindahl-Moreau.

## Clause 1. Property

The Landlord leases to the Tenant Townhouse 58, Falcon Reed Mews, Jumeirah Village Circle, Dubai, a three-bedroom townhouse with a private garden, for residential use.

## Clause 2. Term

The term is one year, from 15 March 2026 to 14 March 2027.

## Clause 3. Rent

The annual rent is AED 145,000, paid by two cheques of AED 72,500, dated 15 March 2026 and 15 September 2026.

## Clause 4. Security deposit

On signing, the Tenant pays a security deposit of AED 7,250, which the Landlord will refund at the end of the term, less the cost of repairing any damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.

## Clause 5. Government fees

The Tenant pays the housing fee that Dubai Municipality charges with the electricity bill, and any other fee that government entities charge for the use of the townhouse.

## Clause 6. Garden and pool

The Tenant maintains the garden. The Landlord pays for the community pool through the service charges.

## Clause 7. Termination by the Landlord

The Landlord may terminate this contract at any time during its term by giving the Tenant 30 days' notice.

## Clause 8. Sale of the townhouse

If the Landlord sells the townhouse, this contract ends on the date of the transfer, and the Tenant shall vacate within 30 days of that date.

## Clause 9. Registration

The Landlord will register this contract with the Real Estate Regulatory Agency through the Ejari system.

## Signatures

Signed in Dubai on 2026-03-01 by Idris Brightwater-Nasr and Kwabena Lindahl-Moreau.
