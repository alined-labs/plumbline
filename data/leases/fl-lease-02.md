## Parties

Landlord (the unit owner): Corwin Altamirano-Hale.

Tenants: Priyanka Osei-Lindqvist and Mateus Osei-Lindqvist, who are married to each other.

## Clause 1. Premises

The Landlord leases to the Tenants Unit 1407 of Seagrass Point, a Condominium, 2600 Tidewater Crescent, Fort Lauderdale, Broward County, Florida (the "Unit"), with its assigned parking space and storage locker.

## Clause 2. Term

The term begins on October 1, 2026 and ends on September 30, 2027. This is the Tenants' first lease of the Unit.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is $2,800 a month, due on the first day of each month.

## Clause 4. Security deposit

The Tenants pay the Landlord a security deposit of $2,800. The Landlord holds it in a separate interest-bearing account at Bluewing Savings Bank, 18 Sandpiper Row, Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and pays the Tenants interest at 5 percent a year, simple interest. The notice required by section 83.49(2), Florida Statutes, is attached as Addendum A.

## Clause 5. Utilities

The Tenants pay for electricity and internet. Water, sewer and basic cable are included in the Association's assessments, which the Landlord pays.

## Clause 6. Condominium documents

The Tenants have received the Declaration of Condominium, the bylaws and the rules of Seagrass Point and agree to follow them. The Declaration requires the Seagrass Point Condominium Association, Inc. (the "Association") to approve every lease of a unit and provides for a lease-approval fee.

## Clause 7. Maintenance and repairs

The Landlord will keep the Unit's appliances, plumbing and air conditioning in working order. The Tenants will report any needed repair promptly.

## Clause 8. Landlord's entry

The Landlord, the Landlord's agents and the Landlord's contractors may enter the Unit at any time, without notice to the Tenants, to make repairs.

## Clause 9. Occupancy

The Unit may be occupied only by the Tenants and their minor children.

## Clause 10. Association approval fee

Before the Association reviews this lease, the Tenants shall pay the Association a lease-approval fee of $150 for each Tenant, $300 in total.

## Clause 11. Renewals

Each time this lease is renewed with the same Tenants, the Tenants shall pay the Association's lease-approval fee again, at $150 for each Tenant.

## Clause 12. Association security deposit

Before moving in, the Tenants shall place with the Association a security deposit equal to two months' rent, $5,600, which the Association holds in escrow against damage to the common elements.

## Clause 13. Rent paid to the Association

If the Landlord becomes delinquent in paying any monetary obligation due to the Association, the Association may demand in writing that the Tenants pay the rent directly to the Association until the Landlord's obligations are paid in full. Rent the Tenants pay to the Association after such a demand counts as rent paid under this lease.

## Signatures

Signed at Fort Lauderdale, Florida, on 2026-09-01, by Corwin Altamirano-Hale, Priyanka Osei-Lindqvist and Mateus Osei-Lindqvist.
