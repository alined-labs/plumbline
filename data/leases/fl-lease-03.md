## Parties

Landlord: Sunmoss Homes Trust, 41 Cypress Lantern Court, Orlando, Florida.

Tenant: Delphine Okonkwo-Ruiz.

## Clause 1. Premises

The Landlord leases to the Tenant the single-family home at 1187 Bramblefinch Hollow Drive, Orlando, Orange County, Florida, with its garage and yard (the "Home").

## Clause 2. Term

The term begins on October 15, 2026 and ends on October 14, 2027.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is $2,450 a month, due on the first day of each month.

## Clause 4. Security deposit

The Tenant pays a security deposit of $2,450, which the Landlord holds in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Gladeview Community Bank, 300 Palmetto Arch, Orlando, Florida, for the Tenant's benefit, without commingling it with other funds. The notice required by section 83.49(2), Florida Statutes, is given in Addendum A.

## Clause 5. Lawn and pool

The Tenant will mow the lawn. The Landlord will pay for a pool service once a week.

## Clause 6. Utilities

The Tenant will open accounts in the Tenant's own name for electricity, water and gas.

## Clause 7. Smoking

No smoking is allowed inside the Home.

## Clause 8. Default in rent

If any rent is unpaid on the third day of the month, the Landlord may end this lease by 24 hours' written notice to the Tenant.

## Clause 9. Landlord's remedies

If the rent is more than five days late, the Landlord may change the locks of the Home and have the water and electricity shut off until the Tenant has paid everything due.

## Clause 10. Alterations

The Tenant will not paint, alter or add fixtures to the Home without the Landlord's written consent.
