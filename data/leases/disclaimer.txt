Legal information, not legal advice. Plumbline compares each clause of your lease with the statutes it has indexed for Dubai, Spain, Colombia and Florida, as they stood on the date the lease was signed. It does not know your whole situation, local rules or court decisions, and it can miss a problem or flag a clause that is lawful. No lawyer has reviewed its findings: read the article each finding cites, and consult a qualified professional before you rely on a finding or act on it.
