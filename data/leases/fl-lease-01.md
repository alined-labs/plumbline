## Parties

Landlord: Pelican Reach Rentals LLC, 900 Mangrove Spur Road, Suite 12, Miami, Florida.

Tenant: Tobias Wren-Calloway.

## Clause 1. Premises

The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 3C at 4410 Gilded Ibis Lane, Miami, Florida (the "Premises"), with one assigned parking space, for use as a private residence only.

## Clause 2. Term

The term begins on September 1, 2026 and ends on August 31, 2027.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is $2,150 a month, due on the first day of each month and payable by bank transfer to the account the Landlord names in writing.

## Clause 4. Security deposit

On signing, the Tenant pays a security deposit of $2,150. The Landlord will hold it in a separate non-interest-bearing account at Coastal Fern Bank, 75 Heron Gate, Miami, Florida, and will not commingle it with other funds. The written notice and disclosure required by section 83.49(2), Florida Statutes, are given in Addendum A, which the Tenant received on signing.

## Clause 5. Return of the deposit

The Landlord will return the security deposit, less any deductions the Landlord considers due, within 60 days after the Tenant vacates. The Landlord need not give the Tenant notice of any deduction before making it.

## Clause 6. Late charge

If the rent is not received by the fifth day of the month, the Tenant will pay a late charge of $75.

## Clause 7. Utilities

The Tenant will pay for electricity, internet and water. The Landlord will pay for trash collection and pest control.

## Clause 8. Use and occupancy

The Premises may be occupied by the Tenant and no more than one other person. No business may be run from the Premises.

## Clause 9. Waiver of statutory rights

The Tenant waives all rights, remedies and notices given to tenants by Part II of Chapter 83, Florida Statutes, including any right to a three-day notice before the Landlord ends this lease for unpaid rent.

## Clause 10. Limitation of the Landlord's liability

The Landlord shall not be liable to the Tenant or the Tenant's guests for any injury, loss or damage to person or property in or about the Premises, whatever its cause, including the Landlord's own negligence or failure to make repairs.

## Clause 11. Maintenance

The Tenant will keep the Premises clean and sanitary and will promptly report any needed repair to the Landlord in writing.

## Clause 12. Pets

No pets are allowed without the Landlord's written consent.

## Clause 13. Renewal

If neither party ends this lease at the end of its term, it continues from month to month. Either party may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given at least 30 days before the end of a monthly period.
