Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written under the law of the Emirate of Dubai (Law No. (26) of 2007, as amended by Law No. (33) of 2008, and Decree No. (43) of 2013 on rent increases). Every person, company, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

## Parties

Landlord: Saffron Dune Holdings L.L.C., Office 1907, Zephyr Arc Tower, Business Bay, Dubai.

Tenant: Anneliese Karamanlis-Doshi.

## Clause 1. Property

The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 2304, Coral Mirage Tower, Dubai Marina, Dubai, a two-bedroom unfurnished apartment with one parking bay, for residential use.

## Clause 2. Term

This contract renews the Tenant's tenancy of the apartment, which ran from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2026 at an annual rent of AED 120,000, for one year, from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2027.

## Clause 3. Rent Index

The parties record that the Rent Index of the Emirate of Dubai gives AED 125,000 as the average annual rent of similar units.

## Clause 4. Rent for the renewed term

The annual rent for the renewed term is AED 132,000, an increase of 10% on the previous rent, paid by four post-dated cheques of AED 33,000 each, dated 1 June 2026, 1 September 2026, 1 December 2026 and 1 March 2027.

## Clause 5. Registration

The Landlord will register this contract with the Real Estate Regulatory Agency through the Ejari system within one week of signing.

## Clause 6. Security deposit

The security deposit of AED 6,000 paid under the previous contract is carried over to this one and is non-refundable.

## Clause 7. Maintenance

The Landlord is responsible for major maintenance of the apartment and its systems. The Tenant is responsible for minor maintenance up to AED 500 a job.

## Clause 8. Utilities

The Tenant pays for electricity, water and district cooling, and keeps the accounts in the Tenant's name.

## Clause 9. Assignment and subletting

The Tenant may not assign this contract or sublet the apartment without the Landlord's written consent.

## Signatures
