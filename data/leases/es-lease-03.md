# Lease of a Dwelling (Contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda)

Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written in English under Spanish law (Ley 29/1994, de Arrendamientos Urbanos). Every person, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

- Lease id: es-lease-03
- Jurisdiction: Spain
- Signed on: 2026-03-10

## Parties

Landlord (arrendador), a natural person: Baltasar Ocampo Lindgren.

Tenant (arrendatario): Mireia Santaella Achebe.

## Clause 1. Dwelling

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the flat at Calle de los Azahares Perdidos 7, 2nd floor A, 41003 Seville, in a building under the horizontal property regime, to be the Tenant's permanent home.

## Clause 2. Term

The lease lasts one year from 15 March 2026, subject to the extensions provided by law.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is EUR 840 a month, paid by bank transfer within the first seven days of each month.

## Clause 4. Deposit (fianza)

On signing, the Tenant pays a deposit (fianza) of one month's rent, EUR 840.

## Clause 5. Recovery of the flat

The Landlord may end this lease at any time by giving the Tenant fifteen days' notice if the Landlord needs the flat for himself or for a member of his family.

## Clause 6. Repairs

All repairs to the flat, including repairs to its structure, roof, plumbing and boiler, are paid by the Tenant.

## Clause 7. Subletting

The Tenant may sublet part of the flat only with the Landlord's prior written consent, and may not sublet the whole flat.

## Clause 8. Community rules

The Tenant will follow the rules of the owners' community of the building.

## Signatures

Signed in Seville on 2026-03-10 by Baltasar Ocampo Lindgren and Mireia Santaella Achebe.
