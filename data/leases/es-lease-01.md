# Lease of a Dwelling (Contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda)

Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written in English under Spanish law (Ley 29/1994, de Arrendamientos Urbanos). Every person, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

- Lease id: es-lease-01
- Jurisdiction: Spain
- Signed on: 2026-02-01

## Parties

Landlord (arrendador), a natural person: Leocadia Marván Etxeberri.

Tenant (arrendatario): Joaquín Ballester Nwosu.

## Clause 1. Dwelling

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the flat at Calle del Alcornoque Dorado 14, 3rd floor B, 28015 Madrid, with a storage room, to be the Tenant's permanent home.

## Clause 2. Term

The lease lasts one year from 1 February 2026, subject to the extensions that article 9 of the Ley de Arrendamientos Urbanos gives the Tenant.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is EUR 1,150 a month, paid by bank transfer within the first seven days of each month.

## Clause 4. Payment on signing

On signing, the Tenant pays three months' rent in advance, EUR 3,450, which the Landlord applies to the rent for February, March and April 2026.

## Clause 5. Deposit (fianza)

On signing, the Tenant pays a deposit (fianza) of two months' rent, EUR 2,300.

## Clause 6. Additional guarantee

The Tenant provides a bank guarantee (aval bancario) for EUR 3,450, three months' rent, valid for the whole lease and its extensions.

## Clause 7. Community fees

The Landlord pays the building's community fees.

## Clause 8. Property tax

The Tenant pays the property tax (IBI) on the flat, which amounts to EUR 418 a year at the date of this lease.

## Clause 9. Agency fee

The Tenant pays the fee of the letting agency that arranged this lease, one month's rent plus VAT.

## Clause 10. Works

The Tenant may not carry out works in the flat without the Landlord's written consent.

## Signatures

Signed in Madrid on 2026-02-01 by Leocadia Marván Etxeberri and Joaquín Ballester Nwosu.
