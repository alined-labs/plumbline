The Landlord lets to the Tenant the apartment at Avenida de los Arrayanes Grises No. 12-40, apartment 502, Bogotá D.C., with one parking space, for use as the Tenant's home.

## Clause 2. Term

The contract lasts twelve months, from 1 June 2026 to 31 May 2027.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is COP 3,200,000 a month, paid within the first five days of each month by transfer to the Landlord's account.

## Clause 4. Utilities and common fees

The Tenant pays the public utilities. The Landlord pays the building's common fees (cuotas de administración).

## Clause 5. Rent increases

The rent increases by 10% every six months, starting in December 2026.

## Clause 6. Deposit

On signing, the Tenant delivers to the Landlord a cash deposit of two months' rent, COP 6,400,000, to guarantee the Tenant's obligations under this contract, which the Landlord returns when the Tenant hands back the apartment.

## Clause 7. Co-signer

The co-signer (codeudor) signs this contract and is jointly liable with the Tenant for every obligation it creates.

## Clause 8. Notices
