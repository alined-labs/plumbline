# Lease of a Dwelling (Contrato de arrendamiento de vivienda)

Synthetic lease for Plumbline's lease checker, written in English under Spanish law (Ley 29/1994, de Arrendamientos Urbanos). Every person, company, address and amount in it is invented. CC BY 4.0.

- Lease id: es-lease-02
- Jurisdiction: Spain
- Signed on: 2026-04-01

## Parties

Landlord (arrendador), a company: Residencial Brezo Alto, S.L., Avenida de los Naranjos Azules 220, 46022 Valencia.

Tenant (arrendatario): Aitana Quiroga Mbeki.

## Clause 1. Dwelling

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the flat at Calle de la Albufera Serena 31, 5th floor, door 12, 46023 Valencia, to be the Tenant's permanent home.

## Clause 2. Term

The lease lasts three years, from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2029, and ends on that date without any extension. The Tenant waives the extensions provided in article 9 of the Ley de Arrendamientos Urbanos.

## Clause 3. Rent

The rent is EUR 980 a month, paid by direct debit on the fifth day of each month.

## Clause 4. Deposit (fianza)

On signing, the Tenant pays a deposit (fianza) of one month's rent, EUR 980, which the Landlord lodges with the regional deposit office.

## Clause 5. Additional guarantee

The Tenant provides an additional guarantee of two months' rent, EUR 1,960, by bank transfer to the Landlord.

## Clause 6. Updating the deposit

The deposit (fianza) is updated on 1 April of every year so that it always equals one month's current rent.

## Clause 7. Repairs

The Landlord carries out the repairs needed to keep the flat habitable. The Tenant pays for small repairs caused by ordinary wear and tear.

## Clause 8. Pets

The Tenant may keep one cat or one small dog.
