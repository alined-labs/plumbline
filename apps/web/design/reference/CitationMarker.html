If your landlord isn't claiming any of the deposit, they have 15 days after you move out to return it . If they want to keep some of it, they must send you written notice by certified mail within 30 days , and you then have 15 days to object in writing .

US-FL Fla. Stat. § 83.49(3)(a) Verified
"You are hereby notified that you must object in writing to this deduction from your security deposit within 15 days from the time you receive this notice or I will be authorized to deduct my claim from your security deposit."
