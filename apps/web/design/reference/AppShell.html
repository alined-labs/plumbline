Deposit return deadline in MiamiUS-FL
My landlord in Miami hasn't returned my deposit. How long do they have?
2 sources verified·Answered in 4.2 s·Trace

If your landlord isn't claiming any of the deposit, they have 15 days after you move out to return it .

If they want to keep some of it, they must send you written notice by certified mail within 30 days , and you then have 15 days to object in writing .
