My landlord in Dubai wants me out when the lease ends so he can sell. How much notice do I get, and can he raise the rent instead?
Partial answer1 source, verified·Trace

He can ask you to leave at the end of the contract because he wants to sell, but only if he tells you the reason at least 12 months before the eviction date, through a notary public or by registered mail .
